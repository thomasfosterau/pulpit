Content localisation (pulpit#56, model (b)): the locale is a level in the version chain. A leaf flagged `localised` declares its translated columns with `defineLocaleLevel` and passes them to `defineSubtype` as `locales`; every other column is shared by all of a node's locales, which also share its history, draft, visibility and publication. `./version-chain` gains the locale level (`LocaleStates`, `LocalePatch`, `applyLocalePatch`, `localesOf`, `diffLocales`; `compare` returns `locales`; `create`, `update` and `restore` take `locales`; `restoreVersion` restores every locale together; `ChainHooks.beforeAppendLocales`). `./filter` gains the `locale` arm and `LOCALE_COLUMN`; `./registry` the `localised` flag. `./subtype` gains `getLocalised(id, locales)`, the locale-fallback read after the node's own gate, write validation of the shared / translated split (`WriteRejected`), and a `locales` pass on `BeforeWriteHook` that fails closed: a locale write through a hook without one is refused. A translated column may not be one the read gate reads. `./testing`'s leaf store takes `localised`, and its chain, leaf and publication fakes read the locale level.
